Section 299 – Culpable Homicide
Whoever causes death by doing an act with the intention of causing death, or with the intention of causing such bodily injury as is likely to cause death, commits the offence of culpable homicide.

Section 300 – Murder
Except in the cases hereinafter excepted, culpable homicide is murder if the act by which the death is caused is done with the intention of causing death.

Section 302 – Punishment for Murder
Whoever commits murder shall be punished with death, or imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine.

Section 304 – Punishment for Culpable Homicide Not Amounting to Murder
Whoever commits culpable homicide not amounting to murder shall be punished with imprisonment for life, or imprisonment up to 10 years, or fine, or both.

Section 307 – Attempt to Murder
Whoever does any act with intention or knowledge to cause death shall be punished with imprisonment up to 10 years and fine.

Section 309 – Attempt to Commit Suicide
Whoever attempts to commit suicide shall be punished with simple imprisonment up to 1 year or fine or both.

Section 323 – Punishment for Voluntarily Causing Hurt
Whoever voluntarily causes hurt shall be punished with imprisonment up to 1 year or fine up to 1000 rupees or both.

Section 324 – Voluntarily Causing Hurt by Dangerous Weapons
Whoever causes hurt using dangerous weapons shall be punished with imprisonment up to 3 years or fine or both.

Section 325 – Voluntarily Causing Grievous Hurt
Whoever causes grievous hurt shall be punished with imprisonment up to 7 years and fine.

Section 326 – Causing Grievous Hurt by Dangerous Weapons
Punishable with imprisonment for life or up to 10 years and fine.

Section 354 – Assault or Criminal Force to Woman
Whoever assaults or uses criminal force against a woman shall be punished with imprisonment up to 5 years and fine.

Section 376 – Rape
Whoever commits rape shall be punished with rigorous imprisonment not less than 10 years which may extend to life imprisonment.

Section 378 – Theft
Whoever dishonestly takes movable property out of possession of any person without consent commits theft.

Section 379 – Punishment for Theft
Punishable with imprisonment up to 3 years or fine or both.

Section 390 – Robbery
Theft becomes robbery when force or fear is used.

Section 392 – Punishment for Robbery
Punishable with rigorous imprisonment up to 10 years and fine.

Section 420 – Cheating and Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property
Punishable with imprisonment up to 7 years and fine.

Section 441 – Criminal Trespass
Entering into property with intent to commit an offence or intimidate is criminal trespass.

Section 447 – Punishment for Criminal Trespass
Punishable with imprisonment up to 3 months or fine or both.
